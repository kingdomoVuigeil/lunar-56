FLIGHT MANUAL

Easy to fly.
Hard to land.

You start 2,500 feet up, falling at 500 ft/s, with 600 lb of fuel. Touch down at less than 5 ft/s.

  1. Choose a burn. Drag the slider, tap a preset, or enter a whole number from 0 to 75.
  2. Fire for one second. Tap BURN. The flight pauses again after each turn.
  3. Watch your descent. Brake early, then ease off as you approach the surface.
0Free fall.
Gain 5 ft/s downward.
5Balance gravity.
Keep your velocity.
75Maximum thrust.
Gain 70 ft/s upward.

The engine can reverse your descent. If you rise, coast to come back down. When your tank is empty, the remaining fall plays out.

The calculator underneath

This adaptation uses the SR-56 game's one-second model: acceleration = burn − 5, velocity += acceleration, and altitude += old velocity + acceleration ÷ 2. Touchdown is resolved at the surface within the final second. Fuel is limited to what is in the tank.

The graph shows altitude or signed velocity over time. Negative velocity means descent. Inspect any recorded point with your finger or mouse.
